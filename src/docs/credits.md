# Credits

Credits are the currency used for AI-powered features in Comic Book Maker. This includes image generation and Story AI assistance.

## How Credits Work

- **Deducted on use**: Credits are deducted when you successfully complete an AI action
- **Monthly allocation**: New credits are added at the start of each billing cycle
- **Credits roll over**: Unused credits carry over to the next month - you never lose what you don't spend

## Checking Your Balance

Your credit balance is displayed in several places:

- **Editor header**: The lightning bolt icon shows your current balance
- **AI Image modal**: Balance shown before generating
- **Story AI panel**: Balance shown in the panel

Click on your credit balance in the editor to view your transaction history.

## Transaction History

View your complete credit history by clicking on your balance in the editor. The history shows:

- **Date and time** of each transaction
- **Credits used** or added
- **Action type** (image generation, story chat, etc.)
- **Details** about the specific action

History is stored for your reference and helps track usage patterns.

## Running Low on Credits

When your balance is low:

1. **Plan your generations** - Use lower-cost models when experimenting
2. **Save prompts** - Reuse successful prompts from history
3. **Wait for your monthly allocation** - New credits are added each billing cycle
4. **Purchase Credits** - Purchase more credits for your account.

If you attempt an action without enough credits, you'll see an "Insufficient Credits" message with the amount needed.

## Tips for Efficient Credit Usage

### Image Generation

- Start with **Nano Banana** (2 credits) for quick tests
- Use **FLUX 2 Dev** (4 credits) for iteration of images
- Reserve **FLUX 2 Pro** (6 credits) for final, high-quality renders
- Save and reuse seeds from images you like

### Story AI

- Be specific in your questions to get useful answers quickly
- Use page capture only when visual context is needed
- Reference character names - the AI already knows them
- Batch related questions in one message when possible

## FAQ

**Do credits expire?**
No! Unused credits roll over to the next month. You never lose credits you don't spend.

**Can I buy more credits?**
Additional credit purchases are coming soon. For now, credits are included with your subscription tier.

**Why was I charged credits for a failed generation?**
Credits are only deducted on successful completions. If an error occurs, no credits are taken.

**Where can I see my next reset date?**
Click on your credit balance to view transaction history, which shows your billing cycle information.
